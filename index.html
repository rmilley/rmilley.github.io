Overview

Players: 4, 6, or 8  |  Ages: 12+  |  Time: 15–30 mins

Fish for cards from the opposing team, paying attention to what other players ask for and what they get. When you think your team has all the cards of one colour, you can “land” them — but you must know exactly who has which cards, or else the other team steals the point!

History of the game

Jig is based on a folk game called "Canadian Fish" (or "Literature"), which originated in India and found a dedicated following among university students in North America. Jig was created to share this addictive little game with the world, introducing a custom deck of nine coloured sets, a four-player variant, and an optional Shark rule for ultra-competitive play. The name was chosen to honour the province of Newfoundland and Labrador: jigging is a traditional fishing method and a treasured part of our cultural heritage.

About the creator

Originally from a tiny island town off the southwest coast of Newfoundland, Rebecca Milley has a PhD in combinatorial game theory and is a professor of mathematics at Grenfell Campus, Memorial University.

About the publisher

Fogberry Games is an independent tabletop game publisher based in western Newfoundland, Canada. Our name is inspired by the bakeapple — also known as cloudberry — a cherished amber fruit which thrives in the foggy bogs of the North Atlantic.
